Meridian Wealth — Company Information

ABOUT
Meridian Wealth is a financial planning and investment advisory firm based in
Kochi (Cochin), Kerala, serving individuals, families, business owners and NRIs
across India and abroad. We help people make calm, well-informed money decisions
— building a clear plan, investing sensibly for the long term, and staying on
track through every stage of life. Our approach is simple: understand your goals
first, then match the strategy to them — never the other way around. (This is a
sample firm used to demonstrate the Haze AI website assistant.)

HOW WE'RE DIFFERENT
- Goal-first, not product-first: we start with your life goals, not a sales pitch.
- Plain-language advice: no jargon, no pressure, everything explained clearly.
- Long-term partnership: regular reviews, not a one-time transaction.
- Transparent about fees and how we're paid.

SERVICES
- Financial Planning: a complete roadmap covering income, expenses, savings,
  goals (home, children's education, retirement) and emergency funds.
- Mutual Fund & SIP Advisory: choosing and reviewing funds suited to your goals
  and risk comfort; setting up and optimising SIPs.
- Retirement Planning: working out how much you need and how to get there, plus
  generating a steady income after you retire.
- Tax-Saving Planning: structuring investments (ELSS, and other options) to be
  tax-efficient under Indian tax rules.
- Goal-Based Investing: education, home purchase, a child's wedding, or building
  long-term wealth — each goal planned and tracked separately.
- Insurance Review: making sure your life and health cover is right — enough,
  but not over-sold.
- NRI Services: investment and financial planning guidance for non-resident
  Indians, including repatriation-friendly options.

WHO WE HELP
Salaried professionals starting to invest, families planning big goals, business
owners, people approaching retirement, and NRIs who want trusted guidance back
home in India.

OUR PROCESS
1. Free discovery call (about 30 minutes): we understand your situation and goals.
2. Plan: we build a clear, written financial plan tailored to you.
3. Implement: we help you put it in place, step by step.
4. Review: regular check-ins to keep the plan on track as life changes.

HOW WE CHARGE
The first discovery consultation is free with no obligation. After that we offer
a simple, transparent fee-based advisory arrangement, explained clearly before
you commit — you'll always know exactly what you're paying and why. There is
never any pressure to proceed.
