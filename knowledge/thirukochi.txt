Thirukochi Financial Services — Company Information

ABOUT
Thirukochi Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. (formerly Thirukochi Financial Consultants
Pvt Ltd) is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund Distributor based in Kochi, Kerala,
serving clients across India. ARN: 81916 (valid 31.01.2011 to 30.01.2029).
CIN: U67190KL2010PTC027180. Mission: to make financial stability and growth
accessible to every Indian through tailored financial services and knowledge.
Investing is 100% paperless, with online SIP/mutual fund purchase, a portfolio
login, and the Thirukochi Financial Education App on the Play Store and App Store.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
- We are a DISTRIBUTOR, not a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser. We do not give
  investment advice, financial planning, goal planning, or recommendations.
- We charge NO fees directly to customers — we earn commission from the product
  providers (e.g. asset management companies).
- Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related
  documents carefully before investing.

PRODUCTS & SERVICES
- Mutual Funds & SIP: equity, debt, hybrid, and index funds; SIPs for regular,
  disciplined investing; online purchase and portfolio tracking.
- Share Broking: equities, derivatives, and commodities on NSE & BSE through our
  partner broker Captocks, with trading platforms and research reports.
- Fixed Deposits & Company Deposits: fixed-term deposits from companies as an
  alternative to bank FDs, with competitive rates and a range of tenures.
- RBI Bonds: government-backed bonds with fixed interest, capital protection, and
  liquidity after an initial lock-in.
- Capital Gain Bonds: reinvest capital gains (e.g. from a property sale) for tax
  exemption under Section 54EC; fixed interest, 3-year lock-in.
- Tax Saving Schemes: tax-efficient investment options (e.g. under Section 80C).
- NPS (National Pension System): Tier-I and Tier-II accounts, choice of pension
  fund managers, Active/Auto investment choice; extra tax benefit under 80CCD(1B).
- Post Office Schemes: government small-savings schemes.
- Life Insurance: term, whole life, endowment, ULIP, and money-back plans; tax
  benefits under Sections 80C and 10(10D); riders like critical illness cover.
- General Insurance: motor, fire, property, and marine insurance, with renewal
  reminders.
- Real Estate: guidance on residential, commercial, and industrial property
  investments with transparent, well-documented transactions.
- PAN Card Services: new PAN applications, corrections, and updates for
  individuals, companies, and NRIs, with online application and tracking.
- TDS Return Filing: timely, accurate quarterly or annual TDS return filing.
- Financial tools: free calculators and financial fitness tools on the website.

PRICING
