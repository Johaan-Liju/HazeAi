ABOUT

iCAD.in is an Autodesk Authorized Training and Certification Centre providing computer-aided design (CAD) training across multiple engineering disciplines. Founded in 2005 as a venture of Covenant Network Technologies Pvt. Ltd., iCAD.in is a premium technology training provider in South India specializing in design technology education and professional certification.

Mission: Quality education using world class technology.

Vision: Providing quality training and end-to-end solutions using world class technologies, enhancing value for customers and exceeding expectations through leading infrastructure, cutting edge training tools, and well-experienced certified trainers.

Key Credentials:
- ISO Certified Company
- Autodesk Authorized Training Center
- Autodesk Authorized Certification Center (Authorized Testing Center)
- Internationally recognized certificates
- State-of-the-art CAD Labs equipped with high-end machines
- Training on Autodesk original software and official courseware
- Well-experienced and certified instructors
- Placement assistance with 95% placement rate

CERTIFICATION & BENEFITS

Autodesk Certification Levels:
1. Autodesk Certified Associate (Entry level, 90 mins, multiple choice)
2. Autodesk Certified Professional (90 mins, 20 performance-based items)
3. Autodesk Certified Expert (Performance-based, 15 specialized questions)

Certification Benefits:
- Industry-recognized validation of skills and knowledge
- Enhanced professional credibility and competitive advantage
- Electronic certificate suitable for printing and framing
- Official Autodesk Certification logo for business cards, resume, letterhead
- Immediate diagnostic feedback on exam performance
- Listing in Autodesk's public Certified Users Database
- Performance-based testing (hands-on skill demonstration)
- Internationally valid and globally recognized

Approximately 94% of iCAD.in trained students achieve certification.
