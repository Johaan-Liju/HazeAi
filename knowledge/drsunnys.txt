Dr. Sunny's Dental Clinic (Company Information)

ABOUT
Dr. Sunny's Dental Clinic is a dental clinic in Kaloor, Kochi (Ernakulam),
Kerala, operating since 2016. It offers a comprehensive range of dental
treatments with an emphasis on advanced technology, painless procedures and
affordability, and is known as one of Kerala's leading Invisalign providers. The
clinic also offers consultant ENT (ear, nose & throat) services and dental
tourism for international patients. It is a single-location clinic (no branches).

SERVICES
- Invisalign & clear aligners
- Orthodontic braces
- Dental implants
- Root canal treatment & fillings
- Teeth whitening
- Crowns, bridges & dentures
- Gum treatment
- Wisdom tooth extraction / removal
- Children's dental care (pedodontics)
- Cosmetic dentistry & smile design
- ENT services (by appointment, via a consultant ENT surgeon)
- Dental tourism for international patients

TECHNOLOGY & STANDARDS
State-of-the-art equipment including an iTero intraoral scanner, dental lasers
and digital radiography. Focus on painless procedures with advanced pain
management, and stringent disinfection and sterilisation protocols.

DENTAL TOURISM
The clinic welcomes international patients who want quality dental care at
affordable prices while visiting Kerala — the full range of treatments (implants,
Invisalign/braces, root canals, whitening, gum treatment, cosmetic work) under
the same hygiene and sterilisation standards. Booking is online or direct; the
team confirms the reservation and sets up the appointment. (No airport pickup,
accommodation or fixed pricing packages are advertised.)
