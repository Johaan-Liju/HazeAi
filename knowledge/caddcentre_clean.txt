ABOUT
CADD Centre is a global network of skill development centres founded in 1988, pioneering Computer Aided Design (CAD), Computer Aided Engineering (CAE), and project management training. Operating for over 38 years, the company has trained 4 million engineers and professionals across 600+ franchise centres in 40+ countries.

CORE BUSINESS
CADD Centre provides industry-aligned, certification-based training in design engineering, automation, IT, creative design, and soft skills. The training serves students, job seekers, working professionals, and corporate clients seeking skill development and employability enhancement.
