Akson Fintech — Company Information

ABOUT
Akson Fintech Advisory Services Private Limited is a full-stack business and
management consulting company based in Vennala, Kochi, Kerala. It was
established in 2014 and was formerly known as Pegasus Business Consultants. The
firm provides end-to-end financial and business consulting solutions to
companies, startups, and foreign investors — covering finance, tax, compliance,
company formation, and legal representation.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
- Akson Fintech is a business and management CONSULTING firm. We are not a SEBI
  Registered Investment Adviser and not an AMFI-registered mutual fund
  distributor — we do not sell investments or give investment advice.

SERVICES
- Virtual CFO: outsourced chief financial officer support for businesses that
  need senior finance expertise without a full-time hire.
- Compliance Management: keeping a business compliant with its statutory
  obligations.
- Tax Planning and Advisory: planning and advice on direct and indirect tax.
- Company Registration: incorporating and registering new companies.
- Startup Advisory Services: guidance for founders and early-stage businesses.
- Foreign Investment Facilitation: helping overseas investors invest into India.
- Corporate Training and Development: training programmes for company teams.
- Legal Representation: representation on the firm's areas of practice.
