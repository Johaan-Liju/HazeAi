Vasupradah Investment Advisory Services Pvt. Ltd. — Company Information

ABOUT
Vasupradah Investment Advisory Services Pvt. Ltd. (VIAPL) is a SEBI-registered
Investment Adviser (RIA) based in Kochi, Kerala, India, servicing Indian
investors across the globe (including NRIs). The name "Vasupradah" is a Sanskrit
word rooted in Indian mythology and associated with financial success, wealth,
prosperity, material abundance, generosity, and philanthropy. The firm helps
individuals and businesses make confident, well-informed financial decisions,
guiding clients through goal-based investing with a structured, personalized
roadmap to wealth creation. Its mission is to move clients toward a safe and
secure financial future through transparency, integrity, planned investment,
client profiling, and curated portfolios backed by fundamental and technical
research. Website: vasupradah.com.

SERVICES
- Investment Advisory (Equity & Mutual Fund) — the firm's core RIA service.
  Internal advisory strategies carry Sanskrit names: Suraksha Vitta (Protection
  of finance), Samana Utpady (Balanced creation), Sthira Sampatti (Stable
  assets), Dirghakalika Nivesa (Long-term investments), and Niyamabadha
  Praksepaka Yojana (Systematic Investment Plan).
- Portfolio Evaluation Service (Punarikshana) — review of an existing portfolio.
- Financial Planning (Vittavyavasthaa) — aligning investments with life goals,
  covering savings adequacy, goal tracking, retirement and independence.
- Retirement Planning (Sevanivruttih Upakrama).
- Vasupradah Wealth / Mutual Fund Distribution (Vasupradah Samriddhi) — a curated
  basket of mutual funds and SIPs with personalized fund selection, transaction
  support, and reporting.
- Training — financial-literacy programmes for workforces.
Clients are matched to a specific service after assessing their risk profile,
investment horizon, and financial goals.

WHO IT SERVES
Retail clients with investments of Rs. 10 lakhs and above, High Net Worth
Indians (in India and abroad), NRIs investing in India, and returning NRIs. The
firm addresses investors at every life stage — Gen Z / new professionals, young
families, midlifers, single mothers, those in their "2nd innings," and retirees.

ADVISORY FEE STRUCTURE
Annual advisory fee (excluding GST), based on investment value, payable
quarterly within 7 days of the invoice date:
- ₹10-15 lakhs: ₹20,000
- ₹15-25 lakhs: ₹35,000
- ₹25-50 lakhs: ₹60,000
- ₹50-75 lakhs: ₹90,000
- ₹75-100 lakhs: ₹1,20,000
- Above ₹100 lakhs: ₹1,51,000
The Portfolio Evaluation Service is offered on a flat fee (approximately
₹12,000). SEBI permits RIAs only two fee types: a fixed fee capped at Rs. 1.51
lakhs per annum, or an asset-based fee capped at 2.5% per annum. The advisory
fee is the only monetary transaction between client and adviser; fee invoices
are sent only by email (from IDs listed in the agreement). No profit-sharing is
permitted.

HOW TO GET STARTED (ONBOARDING)
As a SEBI-registered RIA, the firm follows mandatory onboarding steps:
1. Initial discussion — a recorded Zoom meeting with Principal Officer Jaideep
   Menon to understand goals and explain the model (book via the "Book an
   Appointment" section on the website).
2. Risk profiling — a short online questionnaire assessing risk tolerance and
   suitability.
3. Account opening — completed digitally via the firm's technology platform. For
   a Demat-cum-trading account, clients may choose brokers such as Zerodha,
   Upstox, IIFL, ICICI Direct, Groww, Angel One, and others, and link a bank
   account dedicated to the investments.
4. Signing the Letter of Engagement — a SEBI-mandated agreement signed online
   using Aadhaar OTP; the client receives a copy signed by both parties along
   with the agreed risk profile and a unique client ID.
The investment advisory agreement must be executed and risk profiling completed
before any fee is paid or any advice is given.

RISK & IMPORTANT NOTES
- Investments in securities markets are subject to market risks; read all
  related documents carefully before investing. Past performance is not
  indicative of future results. The firm cannot promise or guarantee any assured,
  risk-free, minimum, or target returns.
- Registration granted by SEBI, membership of BASL, and NISM certification do not
  guarantee performance or assure returns.
- As an RIA, the firm cannot take custody of investor money, cannot transact on a
  client's behalf, and does not accept Power of Attorney. It only provides
  investment advice; all transactions happen only when the client authorises them.
- Beware of impostors: the firm will never ask you to transfer money to any
  account to invest on your behalf, and fee requests are sent only by email from
  the official IDs in your agreement. Verify any adviser's SEBI registration
  number on the SEBI website before transferring funds.
