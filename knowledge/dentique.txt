Dentique — The Dental Studio (Company Information)

ABOUT
Dentique (Dentique – The Dental Studio) is one of the leading dental clinics in
Kochi (Cochin), Ernakulam, Kerala, offering a full spectrum of dental treatments
under one roof. It positions itself as a comprehensive "dental studio" serving
patients from Kochi, Ernakulam, Thrissur and beyond, as well as international
patients through medical tourism. The clinic uses advanced technology — digital
X-rays, laser dental systems, 3D digital scanning and digital smile design — and
follows international hygiene and sterilisation protocols. Two distinctive
features: music therapy to help anxious patients relax, and MICT (Minimal
Invasive Cosmetic Treatments), a concept pioneered by its director for quick,
low-pain makeovers.

VISION & MISSION
Vision: to create a society that is orally healthy and aware of dental hygiene
through quality dental care. Mission: to provide a comprehensive platform for all
dental concerns using the latest technology and high-quality standards, blending
world-class clinical practice with education and research.

SERVICES
- Cosmetic Dentistry: digital smile design, porcelain & composite veneers, teeth
  whitening, gum contouring, composite bonding.
- Orthodontics: metal braces, ceramic/porcelain braces, self-ligating braces,
  Fastbraces, clear aligners, and Invisalign (Dentique is a certified Invisalign
  provider; the director holds Platinum Elite Invisalign Provider status).
- Dental Implants: permanent, natural-looking titanium tooth replacement, planned
  with digital imaging.
- Root Canal Treatment / Endodontics.
- Dental Crowns, Bridges & Dentures / Prosthodontics.
- Tooth Fillings.
- Dentistry for Kids (paediatric dentistry).
- Laser Dentistry.
- Oral Cancer Screening.
- Snoring, Sleep Apnea & Bruxism (teeth grinding) — treated with custom-fitted
  oral appliances.
- Sports Dentistry & Facial Physiotherapy.
- Maxillofacial Surgery & Plastic Surgery.
- Periodontics (gum disease): scaling & root planing, flap surgery, gingivectomy,
  gum grafting, crown lengthening, gum depigmentation and more.
- Smile Design Treatment.

SKIN & HAIR (COSMIQUE SKINCARE)
Dentique also offers dermatology and aesthetic services under "Cosmique
Skincare," staffed by dermatologists and plastic surgeons. Non-surgical:
microdermabrasion, chemical peels, microneedling, mesotherapy, PRP, laser
therapies, photo facial, mole/skin-lesion removal, Botox, dermal fillers, derma
threads, scar revision, ear/nose piercing, hair transplant. Surgical:
blepharoplasty, liposuction/body contouring, rhinoplasty, abdominoplasty, breast
reconstruction.

MEDICAL TOURISM
Dentique welcomes patients from across India and abroad. Its director founded
Providence Medicare, a medical-tourism facilitation firm in Kerala. Treatments
such as Invisalign/clear aligners and MICT cosmetic makeovers can be completed
within a short stay in Kerala.
