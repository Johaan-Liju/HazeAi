Progress Planners — Company Information

ABOUT
Progress Planners is a financial advisory group that describes itself as "a
synonym for ethical financial advisory." It was formed on the principle that
every client deserves inexpensive, tax-efficient, transparent, customised and
superior portfolios that work seamlessly with their financial plan to achieve
their goals in life. The group serves over 400 families and institutions through
offices in Kochi, Bangalore and Chennai.

HOW WE ARE STRUCTURED (IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES)
Progress Planners deliberately keeps investment advisory separate from product
distribution, so that advice is never influenced by product commissions. This
follows SEBI regulations and global best practice. There are two distinct
entities under the Progress Planners brand:
- Progress Planners Financial Services — a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser
  (SEBI Registration No: INA 200004672). It provides comprehensive, FEE-BASED
  financial planning: investments, risk management, tax, retirement and estate
  planning, for individuals, families and institutions.
- Progress Planners Financial Solutions LLP — an independent wealth management
  firm that DISTRIBUTES mutual fund products (AMFI Registration No: ARN-102672).
Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related
documents carefully before investing.

PRICING
The advisory entity works on a FEE-BASED model rather than commissions; the
distribution entity earns commission from product providers. Specific fees are
not published on the website — contact us and we will explain the fee structure
for your situation.
