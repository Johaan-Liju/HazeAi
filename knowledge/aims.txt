AIMS Insurance Broking Company Pvt. Ltd. — Company Information

ABOUT
AIMS Insurance Broking Company Pvt. Ltd. is an IRDAI-licensed insurance broker
(licensed since 2003) headquartered in Thrissur, Kerala, serving clients across
South India for 22+ years. ISO 9001:2015 certified. Numbers at a glance:
1,00,000+ policies managed, 10,000+ clients advised, 45+ certified insurance
professionals with 500+ years of combined expertise, 36+ insurance partners,
and 15+ branches across South India.
Mission: to guide individuals, families, and businesses toward smart insurance
decisions through expert advice, personalized service, and deep risk
understanding. Vision: to be South India's most trusted insurance partner.

CORE SERVICES
- Risk Management: spot risks early and suggest the right coverage to protect
  your business and personal belongings.
- Policy Gap Analysis: review your current policies to find missing coverage
  and remove duplicate, expensive premiums.
- Comprehensive Claim Management: experts handle claims end-to-end, dealing
  with insurers, tracking progress, and maximizing your settlement.
- Claims Consultancy: professional help with paperwork and complete
  submissions for faster, better settlement outcomes.
- Insurance Portfolio Management: organize and monitor all your policies
  together, keeping the best protection at the best price.
- Customized Insurance Solutions: personal plans for individuals, families,
  and businesses that fit your needs and budget.

INSURANCE PRODUCTS
- Individual: Health, Life, Motor, Home, Personal Accident, Travel.
- Group plans: Group Health (Group Mediclaim), Group Personal Accident,
  Group Term Life.
- Business: Fire, Marine, Liability, Commercial, Engineering, Miscellaneous.
- Rural/Agricultural: Dairy Insurance, Agricultural Insurance, and SHG
  (Self-Help Group) Coverage.
As a broker, AIMS works with 36+ insurance companies and compares options to
recommend what fits the client — it is not tied to a single insurer.
