Wealth Matrix — Company Information

ABOUT
Wealth Matrix Finserv LLP is a certified financial planning firm on MG Road,
Ernakulam, Kochi, Kerala. The firm develops smart, results-driven approaches to
turn a client's financial goals into reality — covering mutual funds,
retirement, a child's education, travel, and insurance. Clients get a portfolio
login and a mobile app so they can track their investments and SIPs on demand.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
- Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related
  documents carefully before investing.
- The website publishes our commission disclosures, the AMFI Code of Conduct,
  and our investor grievance redressal process.

SERVICES
- Mutual Fund Planning: choosing and managing mutual fund investments, including
  SIPs, with ongoing portfolio monitoring and tracking.
- Retirement Planning: building a corpus and a plan for life after work.
- Child Education Planning: planning and investing towards a child's education
  goals.
- Insurance Planning: matching life and general insurance cover to the family's
  needs.
- Travel Planning: planning and saving towards travel goals.

ONLINE ACCESS
- Client portal: wealthmatrix.my-portfolio.co.in — monitor your portfolio, SIPs,
  and goals.
- Mobile app: the Wealth Matrix app is available on Google Play and the Apple
  App Store.
- The website carries 15+ free calculators, including SIP, lumpsum, retirement,
  education, life insurance, EMI, marriage, house, car and vacation planners.
