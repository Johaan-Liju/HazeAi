Kanz Wealth — Company Information

ABOUT
Kanz Wealth is a financial advisory firm based in Palarivattom, Kochi, Kerala,
founded in 2016. It focuses on ethical, personalised financial advice covering
mutual funds, insurance, retirement planning, and Shariah-compliant investment
solutions, for both resident Indian and NRI clients. The firm reports 10+ years
of experience, 10,000+ clients consulted, and 5,000+ insurance customers
assisted. In the founder's words: "Personal finance is deeply personal to each
and every one of you."

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
- Kanz Wealth is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund DISTRIBUTOR. ARN: 117786, valid
  till 04-12-2028.
- Registered with APMI as a PMS Adviser. APRN: 06865.
- Registered sub-broker with NSE and BSE.
- Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related
  documents carefully before investing.

SERVICES
- Investment Planning: goal-based portfolios, mutual funds, SIP, ELSS, equity.
- Retirement Planning: SWP, NPS, PPF, and FIRE (financial independence)
  strategies.
- Estate Planning: wills, trusts, succession planning, and Faraid (Islamic
  inheritance) guidance.
- NRI Wealth Management: NRE/NRO planning, DTAA tax planning, and repatriation
  guidance.
- PMS & AIF: Portfolio Management Services, Alternate Investment Funds, pre-IPO
  and REIT options.
- Family Financial Planning: education SIPs, home loans, and tax planning.
- Insurance Services: health, term life, critical illness, maternity, motor,
  travel, home, and business cover.
- Claim Support: full advocacy from documentation through to settlement.
