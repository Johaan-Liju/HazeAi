Brightview Dental Studio — Clinic Information

ABOUT
Brightview Dental Studio is a modern, full-service dental clinic in Kochi
(Cochin), Ernakulam, Kerala. We bring general, cosmetic and specialist dentistry
together under one roof, with a calm, patient-first approach — gentle care,
clear pricing, and no upselling. From a routine cleaning to a full smile makeover,
our aim is simple: healthy teeth, an easy visit, and a treatment plan you actually
understand. (This is a sample clinic used to demonstrate the Haze AI website
assistant.)

WHY PATIENTS CHOOSE US
- Same-week appointments and short waiting times.
- Transparent, upfront pricing — you know the cost before treatment begins.
- Painless dentistry: local anaesthetic done gently, plus sedation options for
  anxious patients.
- Digital X-rays and 3D scanning for accurate, low-radiation diagnosis.
- Strict international sterilisation and hygiene protocols.
- Friendly team that explains every option in plain language.

SERVICES
- General Dentistry: check-ups, scaling & polishing (cleaning), tooth fillings,
  tooth extractions, gum care.
- Root Canal Treatment (single and multi-sitting).
- Dental Crowns, Bridges & Dentures.
- Dental Implants: permanent titanium tooth replacement, planned with 3D imaging.
- Cosmetic Dentistry: teeth whitening, composite bonding, veneers, smile design.
- Orthodontics (braces & aligners): metal braces, ceramic braces, and clear
  aligners for adults and teens.
- Paediatric Dentistry: gentle care for children, fluoride treatment, sealants.
- Wisdom Tooth Removal and minor oral surgery.
- Emergency Dentistry: same-day relief for toothache, broken teeth, and swelling.

INDICATIVE PRICING (INR)
Prices are guidelines; the dentist confirms your exact cost after an examination.
- First consultation & check-up: FREE (includes a digital X-ray if needed).
- Scaling & polishing (cleaning): from ₹1,200.
- Tooth filling: from ₹800.
- Root canal treatment: ₹4,000 – ₹8,000 depending on the tooth.
- Tooth extraction: from ₹1,000 (wisdom tooth from ₹3,500).
- Dental crown: from ₹5,000 (metal-ceramic) to ₹14,000 (full ceramic/zirconia).
- Dental implant: from ₹28,000 per tooth (implant + crown).
- Teeth whitening: from ₹8,000.
- Braces: from ₹30,000; clear aligners from ₹1,20,000.
We offer EMI / easy-instalment options on treatments above ₹20,000.

Digital X-rays (up to 80% less radiation), intra-oral cameras so you see what we
see, 3D digital scanning (no messy moulds), and single-use disposables with
autoclave sterilisation for every instrument.

APPOINTMENTS & BOOKING
Appointments can be booked right here in this chat — just say you'd like to book

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
- Is the first visit really free? Yes — the first consultation and check-up are
  free, including a digital X-ray if the dentist needs one.
- Does a root canal hurt? No. It's done under local anaesthetic; most patients
  say it feels like a normal filling, and any soreness afterwards settles quickly.
- Do you treat children? Yes, we have a paediatric dentist and a child-friendly
  approach.
- Do you offer EMI? Yes, easy instalments are available on treatments above
  ₹20,000.
- I'm nervous about dentists — can you help? Absolutely. We go slowly, explain
  everything, and offer sedation options for anxious patients.
- Do you handle emergencies? Yes — call us for same-day relief for toothache,
  swelling, or a broken tooth.
