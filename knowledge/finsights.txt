Finsights by Square League — Company Information

ABOUT
Finsights ("Financial markets simplified") is the financial education and
insights platform of Square League Wealth Management, a wealth management firm
based in Kochi, Kerala. Finsights publishes articles, courses, quizzes, and
free financial planning tools that make Indian financial markets easy to
understand, while the parent firm Square League provides investment guidance,
financial consulting, and wealth management services.

WHAT'S ON FINSIGHTS
- Articles: analysis of Indian markets and personal finance — mutual funds,
  IPOs, equities, RBI policy and inflation, sector deep-dives (healthcare,
  ports, autos/EVs, semiconductors), and personal finance themes. Filterable
  by category (Personal Finance, Mutual Funds, IPO, Equities, and more).
- Courses / Challenges: guided learning programs, including "The Mutual Fund
  Universe" — a free program on mutual funds.
- Quizzes: interactive tests to check financial knowledge.
- Free tools (downloadable, no signup or payment required):
  - Inflation-Adjusted Goal Calculator: real number of years your savings and
    SIPs need to grow to beat inflation.
  - Goal Horizon Multi-Asset: years your current mix of equity, debt, gold and
    other assets needs to hit your target corpus.
  - EMI Savings Calculator: interest saved and how much faster a loan closes
    by paying a little extra each month.
  - Dream Car Finder: the car you can truly afford based on EMI capacity and
    loan preferences.
  - Retirement Freedom Planner: how much to invest now for an ideal retirement
    corpus and monthly income for life.
  - SIP Booster Calculator: how increasing your SIP yearly boosts long-term
    wealth.
  - Smart Income Generator: plan a steady monthly income from investments with
    a year-by-year breakdown.
  - Goal Achiever Timeline: how long to reach your target wealth at your
    current investment rate.
