CoverFirst — Company Information

ABOUT
CoverFirst is an IRDAI-licensed Insurance Marketing Firm — an independent insurance
advisor serving individuals, families, and businesses across Kerala and Pan India.
We compare plans from 20+ insurers to recommend the best fit (never biased by
commission) and support customers through claims and renewals. Our mission is to
"protect what matters most by delivering reliable, personalized insurance solutions."

WHY US
10+ years' advisory experience · 20+ insurer partners · 500+ clients served ·
98% claims success rate. Insurer partners include LIC, HDFC ERGO, HDFC Life, ICICI,
Star Health, Niva Bupa, Care, Bajaj, Tata AIG, and Digit, among others.

RETAIL INSURANCE (individuals & families)
- Motor (car & two-wheeler): comprehensive, third-party, and own-damage cover;
  zero-depreciation add-on; cashless garage network; 24/7 roadside assistance;
  no-claim-bonus protection.
- Health: individual, family floater, and senior-citizen plans; cashless treatment
  at 10,000+ hospitals; pre- & post-hospitalisation; maternity/newborn cover on
  family plans; senior entry up to age 80; pre-existing conditions covered after a
  waiting period.
- Cyber: cover for identity theft, online fraud, phishing, data loss, social-media
  liability, and cyberbullying.
- Travel: domestic & international trip cover — medical emergencies abroad, emergency
  evacuation, baggage loss, and trip cancellation; Schengen-compliant plans available.
- Life: term plans (cover up to ₹10 crore+), savings/investment plans (endowment,
  ULIP, money-back), and pension/annuity plans; tax benefits under Sec 80C & 10(10D).

CORPORATE & SME INSURANCE
Property (fire, burglary, machinery, stock); employee benefits (group medical,
personal accident, gratuity, group term life); liability (D&O, professional
indemnity, public liability, cyber); marine/transit (cargo, hull, inland);
engineering/project (contractors' all-risk, erection, machinery breakdown); and
specialty/SME packages for healthcare, education, and retail.
