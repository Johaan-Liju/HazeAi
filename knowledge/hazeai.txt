ABOUT
Haze AI puts an AI chat assistant on your business's website. The assistant reads
your website, answers your customers' questions instantly, 24/7, in your
business's own words — and when a visitor wants a callback, it captures their
phone number and emails it to you on the spot. Built for small and medium
businesses in India. Website: hazeai.in

HOW IT WORKS (3 steps)
1. We read your website. Haze AI learns your services, prices, hours and contact
   details from your own pages, so it answers the way you would.
2. Your web person pastes ONE line of code into your site. That is the entire
   installation — it works on any website platform (WordPress, Wix, Shopify,
   custom-built, anything that can include a script tag).
3. Done. The assistant answers visitors day and night. When someone asks for a
   callback, their phone number and question are emailed to you immediately.

KEY FEATURES
- Answers instantly, 24/7 — including nights, weekends and holidays.
- Grounded: it answers ONLY from your own business information. It never invents
  answers. If it doesn't know something, it says so honestly and offers to take
  the visitor's phone number so you can follow up.
- Lead capture: visitor phone numbers are emailed to the business owner the
  moment they're left — while the visitor is still interested.
- Matches your brand: the widget takes your brand colour and greets visitors in
  your company's name.
- Nothing for visitors to download; no app for the business to install; no
  change to your website's design.
- The conversation continues where the visitor left off if they reload the page.
- Mobile friendly — works on phones, tablets and desktops.
