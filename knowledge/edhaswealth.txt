Edhas Wealth — Company Information

ABOUT
Edhas Wealth Private Limited is a one-stop investment boutique in Kochi, Kerala,
offering Mutual Funds, PMS and AIF as part of individual financial goal
fulfilment. The firm helps investors plan around life goals such as retirement
and a child's future, and gives every client an online portfolio login to track
their investments. Edhas Wealth reports 2,500+ happy customers, 25+ years of
experience, 80+ awards, and partnerships with 40+ asset management companies.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURES
- Edhas Wealth is an AMFI-registered Mutual Fund DISTRIBUTOR. ARN: 269697,
  registered 25-05-2023, validity shown as 24-05-2026.
- We earn trailing commission on regular plans only.
- Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks; read all scheme-related
  documents carefully before investing.

PRODUCTS & SERVICES
- Mutual Funds: goal-based investing across schemes from 40+ AMC partners, with
  online investing and a portfolio login.
- PMS (Portfolio Management Services): professionally managed portfolios for
  larger investment amounts.
- AIF (Alternate Investment Funds): alternate investment options beyond
  conventional mutual funds.
- Unlisted Shares: access to shares of companies not listed on the exchanges.
- Offshore Products: investment options outside India.
- Goal planning: dedicated Retirement Savings and Child Future Savings planning.
- KYC and FATCA assistance for new and existing investors.
- Mutual fund forms and downloads.
- Schedule a meeting with an advisor through the website.
