ABOUT IFIHOMES
IFIHomes (ifihomes.com) is one of India's leading online destinations for smart,
innovative home and office automation products — covering lifestyle, health,
safety, security and surveillance. Operating since 2014, it sells under two
brands: IFITech (smart lighting, solar lighting, smart water faucets, home/office/
shop security & surveillance, and home automation) and SeTracker (Connect2Kidz
GPS/LBS smartwatches for children and the elderly, plus GPS trackers that can also
be used for pet monitoring). IFIHomes ships within India only.

DELIVERY & SHIPPING
- Delivery is within India only. International shipping is not offered.
- Orders are processed within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation.
  Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed the next working day.
- Estimated delivery: metro cities 2–5 business days; other locations 5–8 business days.
- Free shipping on all orders above ₹999. A ₹99 shipping fee applies below ₹999.
- Tracking details are sent by email/SMS, and orders can be tracked under
  'My Orders' on the website.
- The courier attempts delivery up to 3 times before returning the package.
- Inspect the package on delivery; refuse it if tampered or damaged and inform us.
  Report any missing items within 24 hours of delivery.

PAYMENT
- Accepted: Razorpay, bank transfer, UPI & net banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
- COD is available for selected pin codes and products, on orders above ₹599.
  It requires a 10% advance of the invoice amount (non-refundable, covers forward
  and return logistics); the remaining 90% is paid at delivery.
- A tax invoice is provided with every order. To claim GST credit, share your GST
  details within 48 hours of purchase.
- For bulk or B2B orders, email care@ifihomes.com to request a proforma invoice.

RETURNS & REFUNDS
- Products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if unused, in original
  packaging, and in the same condition as received.
- Not returnable: installed or used products, items missing original packaging or
  accessories, customized/special-order items, and SD cards/accessories. Items
  damaged by misuse, tampering, or improper installation are not eligible.
- To start a return, contact care@ifihomes.com or call 080-68393434 / 35 with your
  order number and details of the issue.
- Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days — to the original payment
  method for prepaid orders, or by bank transfer/UPI for COD orders. The 10% COD
  advance is non-refundable.
- Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped; after shipping, use the return
  process instead.

WARRANTY
- Products must be registered on the IFIHomes website within 10 days of delivery,

- Warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use; the period varies by

  damage, misuse, unauthorized repairs, or consumables like batteries and SD cards.
- To claim: WhatsApp +91-8951934341 or email care@ifihomes.com with the issue and
  proof of purchase. Servicing takes about 7–10 working days. Warranty is valid
