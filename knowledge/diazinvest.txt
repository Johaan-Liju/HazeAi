ABOUT
Diaz Invest PVT LTD is a financial services provider based in Kerala, India,
operating since 2007. The company positions itself as the "No.1 Mutual Fund
Retail Distributor in Kerala," serving thousands of families with clients
across 50+ countries. It is an AMFI Registered Mutual Fund Distributor.

The company's core purpose is to "EMPOWER PEOPLE TO LIVE WITH PRIDE" and its
central theme is "Converting Savers into Investors." Diaz Invest emphasizes
personalized guidance to help clients "make your dreams a reality by guiding

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
- Why invest through Diaz Invest? 15 years of fund management experience, a
  dedicated relationship manager for each customer, and support to keep
  investing consistently through market volatility.
- What if the mutual fund company shuts down? SEBI regulations prevent closures
  without mergers or sales to other entities. Investor valuations remain
  unaffected; only the fund name may change (e.g., Reliance to Nippon India).
- Direct fund vs. regular fund? Direct funds are bought from the mutual fund
  company and suit self-managing investors with time to manage them. Regular
  funds are bought through a distributor and include professional guidance.
- How are investments monitored? Clients use the Diaz Invest mobile app and
  website portal after investing.
- Can changes be made after starting? Yes — mutual funds offer flexibility and
  liquidity. SIPs can be increased, decreased, or paused anytime (except during
  a lock-in period or for closed-ended funds).
