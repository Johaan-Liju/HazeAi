Affluenz Wealth Advisors — Company Information

ABOUT
Affluenz Wealth Advisors (affluenzwealth.com) is a SEBI-registered, fee-only
investment advisory firm based in Kochi, Kerala, India, operating since March
2014. Its philosophy, "Investing in Life," reflects a client-centric focus on
life goals and the highest standards of fiduciary responsibility. The firm
provides wealth management advice through personalized service and expert
financial counseling to High Net Worth families and corporates. Because it is
independent and fee-only, it aims to give unbiased, objective advice with the
client's interest placed ahead of its own. Its stated focus is to optimise
clients' life goals, not to maximise investments.

FEE-ONLY, INDEPENDENT MODEL
Affluenz is a true fee-only adviser: it does not sell products and receives no
commissions, incentives, or revenue-sharing from third parties. It does not
sell insurance or other distribution products and advises direct (non-
commission) plans only. Disclosure: the adviser's spouse is a major stakeholder
in a private limited financial distribution company; all of the adviser's
investments are made in direct/non-commission mode, and any historical Assets
Under Advice held in distribution mode carry no fees.

FEES, BILLING & REFUNDS
Advisory fees are billed quarterly in arrears — invoices are raised only after
the quarter's services have been fully delivered, so no advance payment is
required and no refunds are offered (the work for each billing period is already
complete when billed). If, by agreement, fees are ever collected in advance,
the advance cannot exceed two quarters; on premature termination the client is
refunded proportionate fees for the unexpired period, and the adviser may retain
a breakage fee of at most one quarter's fee. Fees are payable by cheque, bank
transfer, UPI, or (optionally) the BSE-managed Centralised Fee Collection
Mechanism (CeFCoM). Cash is not accepted. The adviser never holds client funds
or securities and cannot execute any trade without the client's explicit consent
on each trade. Voluntary termination of the advisory agreement requires 30 days'
prior written notice by either party.
