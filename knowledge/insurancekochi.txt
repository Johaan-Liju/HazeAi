CLAIMS SUPPORT
The firm assists clients through the full claim process. For a motor claim, the
general steps are: (1) inform the insurer — call Insurances Kochi at
+91 93888 26733 or the provider's toll-free number immediately; (2) document the
damage with photos/videos; (3) file an FIR if required (theft, third-party
damage, or major accidents); (4) submit the claim form with documents such as RC,
policy copy, and ID proof; (5) a surveyor assesses and approves the claim;
(6) repair at a cashless network garage or opt for reimbursement.

COMMON QUESTIONS
- Vehicle insurance renewal documents: RC, previous policy details, and ID proof.
- Third-party vs comprehensive car insurance: third-party (mandatory by law)
  covers damage to others; comprehensive adds cover for your own vehicle against
  accidents, theft, fire, and natural disasters.
- Health insurance premiums qualify for tax deductions under Section 80D of the
  Income Tax Act; term-insurance premiums also carry tax benefits.
- Term vs whole life: term is pure life cover for a set period (death benefit
  only, no maturity value); whole life covers the entire lifetime and may include
  a savings/investment component.
- Best time to buy life/term insurance: the earlier the better — younger age
  means lower premiums and longer coverage.
