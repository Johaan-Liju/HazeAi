SERVICES

Medical Dermatology (diagnosis and treatment of skin conditions):
- Acne / pimples — the most common skin disease in adolescence, often continuing
  into adulthood (about 20% of men, 35% of women). Treated with topical and oral
  medications, chemical peels, lasers, vacuum therapy, iontophoresis, LED light
  therapy, customised medifacials, and acne injections (for a blemish before an
  important event). The goal is to clear acne and minimise scars, open pores and
  pigmentation.
- Corns — hardened layers of dead skin caused by repeated friction/pressure,
  usually on the soles and palms. Treated by filing/pumice softening or, when
  needed, minor surgical removal under local anaesthesia.
- Skin tags — small, benign, painless growths of blood vessels and collagen.
  Removed by cauterisation, ligation, electrosurgery or laser under topical
  anaesthesia (patients are advised never to remove them at home).
- Skin allergies — including eczema in children (atopic/contact/seborrheic/
  nummular dermatitis, pityriasis alba), pregnancy-related skin diseases, and
  skin diseases in the elderly (geriatric group).
- Vitiligo — an autoimmune loss of skin pigment (generalised, segmental or
  localised/focal). Treated with oral and topical medications, micropigmentation/
  tattooing, PUVA (psoralen + light) phototherapy, and depigmentation for
  widespread cases.
- Psoriasis — a chronic, non-infectious autoimmune condition with thick, silvery-
  white scaly plaques (knees, elbows, scalp, trunk, nails). Treated with medicated
  body washes and shampoos, topical and oral medications, injections and light
  therapy; blood tests or a skin biopsy may precede treatment.
- Pigmentation — hyper- or hypo-pigmentation (age spots, freckles, melasma,
  tanning, sunspots). Treated with topical medications and sunscreens,
  microdermabrasion, laser (Revlite Q-switched Nd:YAG), IV nutritional therapy,
  oral medications, mesotherapy, chemical peeling and PRP.
- Keloid — overgrown, raised scar tissue that can be larger than the original
  wound. Treated with corticosteroid injections, silicone gel/pressure pads,
  laser and cryotherapy.
- Skin infections — fungal (ringworm, athlete's foot, tinea versicolor,
  onychomycosis, candidiasis, etc.), bacterial (cellulitis, folliculitis,
  impetigo, abscess, boils) and viral (warts, molluscum contagiosum, chickenpox,
  shingles) infections, treated with topical, oral or IV medication as indicated.

Cosmetic Dermatology:
- Cryotherapy — safe, simple in-OPD freezing (liquid nitrogen) to remove benign
  and malignant skin lesions.
- Vampire face lift — uses growth factors from the patient's own blood for
  healthy, youthful, rejuvenated skin; a quick outpatient procedure.
- Double chin reduction — non-surgical reduction of excess submental fat.
- Thread lift — resorbable PDO/caprolactone threads lift saggy, dull, tired skin
  and enhance facial contours without visible scars.
- Eye bag reduction — treats under-eye puffiness and dark circles.
- Botox & fillers — Botox relaxes wrinkle-causing muscles; hyaluronic-acid dermal
  fillers restore lost volume.
- Skin polishing — microdermabrasion that exfoliates the top skin layer with
  fine aluminium microcrystals.
- Chemical peels — remove dead cells and debris for clearer, brighter, rejuvenated
  skin, with pore and fine-line reduction.
- Medifacials — a wide range of dermatologist-designed medical facials for dull
  skin, acne, scars, melasma, dryness, hyperpigmentation, rosacea, fine lines,
  sagging, oily or sensitive skin, each planned after a skin analysis.

Laser Dermatology:
- Laser hair reduction / unwanted hair removal — for face, hands, feet, back and
  more; addresses hair from hormonal imbalance, medications, pregnancy or heredity.
- Full-body laser treatments — comprehensive hair reduction planned by section,
  popular with both men and women.
- Tattoo removal — Q-switched Nd:YAG (Revlite) laser fades/removes a wide range of
  inks and colours, including cosmetic and traumatic tattoos; multiple sessions
  under numbing cream.
- Scar treatment — including acne scars, with topical/oral medication, peels,
  lasers, injections and LED therapy.
- Skin tightening — for sagging skin caused by collagen loss, sun exposure,
  lifestyle or poor nutrition.
- Stretch marks — laser treatment for linear stretch-mark scars (pregnancy, weight
  change, growth spurts, steroid overuse).

Hair Treatments:
- Hair dye allergy — management of allergic reactions (mainly to PPD in dyes/
  henna) that cause itching, redness, swelling and blisters; includes patch-test
  guidance and safer-dye advice.
- Hair growth factors / hair-loss treatment — hair mesotherapy (vitamins,
  minerals and amino acids injected into the scalp), PRP for hair regrowth
  (platelet-rich plasma from the patient's own blood, 4–6 monthly sessions),
  low-level light therapy (LLLT, FDA-approved), and Regenera Activa — described as
  Kerala's first regenerative cell therapy for androgenetic alopecia, using the
  patient's own progenitor cells to reactivate dormant follicles.
