Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Haze AI (“Haze AI”, “we”, “us”, “our”) provides AI assistants that businesses add to their website and WhatsApp to answer customer questions, capture enquiries, and book appointments. This Privacy Policy explains what information we handle when you interact with a Haze AI assistant — on a business’s website or through WhatsApp — and how we use and protect it. By messaging a Haze AI assistant, you agree to this Policy.

Who we are

Haze AI is operated from Kochi, Kerala, India. Website: https://hazeai.in. You can reach us at johaanliju@gmail.com.

The information we handle

  • Messages you send. The questions and messages you type to the assistant.
  • Contact details you provide. If you ask to be contacted or to book, we collect your name, phone number, and any details you share (such as your enquiry).
  • Appointment details. If you book, the time slot you choose, along with your name and phone number.
  • WhatsApp details. If you message a business through WhatsApp, your WhatsApp phone number and profile name as provided to us by WhatsApp.
  • Technical information. Basic data such as your IP address and timestamps, used to keep the service secure and prevent abuse (for example, rate-limiting).

We do not ask for sensitive personal information, and you should not share passwords, card numbers, or similar sensitive data in chat.

How we use it

  • To understand your question and generate a helpful reply.
  • To pass your enquiry, contact details, or booking to the business you contacted, so they can follow up.
  • To create appointments on the business’s calendar when you choose to book.
  • To keep the service running, secure, and free from abuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • The business you contacted. Your enquiry, contact details, and bookings are sent to that business so they can respond. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy practices.
  • AI processing — Anthropic. Your messages are processed by Anthropic’s Claude AI to generate replies.
  • Messaging — Meta / WhatsApp. If you contact a business through WhatsApp, your messages are delivered via the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta), subject to WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy.
  • Scheduling — Google. Bookings are written to the business’s Google Calendar using Google’s APIs.
  • Email delivery. We use an email service to deliver your enquiry to the business.

These providers process data on our or the business’s behalf to deliver the service.

How long we keep it

  • Conversation content is held only transiently to maintain the flow of your chat; Haze AI does not store it in a long-term database.
  • Enquiries and bookings passed to a business are retained by that business according to their own policies.
  • Security-related technical data is kept only as long as needed for that purpose.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information in transit (for example, encrypted HTTPS connections) and to limit access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at johaanliju@gmail.com. Because enquiries are also held by the business you contacted, you may need to contact them directly as well.

Children

Haze AI assistants are intended for adults transacting with businesses and are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised, and significant changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact us
