Quick Answers

Your Questions, Answered

From choosing the right policy to making a claim — answered simply and clearly.

What is the difference between comprehensive and third-party motor insurance? +

Comprehensive covers both your own vehicle damage AND third-party liability. Third-party only covers damages to others — it's mandatory by law but doesn't cover your own vehicle's repair costs.

How do I make a health insurance claim? +

For cashless claims, get admitted at a network hospital and show your policy card. For reimbursement, pay the bills, collect all documents, and submit to us — we'll coordinate with the insurer on your behalf.

Is term life insurance better than endowment plans? +

Term insurance offers the highest cover at the lowest cost — ideal for pure protection. Endowment plans combine insurance with savings. The right choice depends on your financial goals and family needs.

Do I need travel insurance for domestic trips in India? +

While not mandatory, domestic travel insurance protects against trip cancellations, flight delays, baggage loss and medical emergencies while travelling. It's affordable and often worth it for multi-city trips.

What is cyber insurance and do I need it? +

Cyber insurance covers financial losses from online fraud, identity theft, phishing attacks and data breaches. With digital payments and online banking now mainstream, it's increasingly important for individuals and families.

How do I become an Insurance Sales Partner (ISP) with Cover First? +

Register via our ISP page, complete the free IRDAI-approved training, get certified and start selling. There's zero investment required. You earn commission on every policy sold and get full support from our team.
